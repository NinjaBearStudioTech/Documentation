# Integrations
<primary-label ref="integrations"/>

All Ninja Bear Studio plugins are designed to work together as part of a larger gameplay ecosystem. You can combine them around the feature you want to build. For example, to make a guard lock on to rivals and let teammates pass, use Ninja Factions to [decide whom the guard notices](integration_bot_factions.md), then connect Ninja Bot to Ninja Combat to [lock on when the guard spots a rival](integration_bot_combat.md).
